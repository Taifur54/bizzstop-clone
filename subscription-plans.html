Frequently Asked Questions

What are contact reveals?
Contact reveals allow you to access the email and phone number of business opportunity posters. Each reveal uses one credit from your account.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your reveals will remain available until the end of your billing period.
Do unused reveals roll over?
For one-time packages, yes! Your reveals never expire. For monthly subscriptions, reveals reset each month but don't roll over.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Absolutely! You can change your plan at any time from your profile dashboard. Changes take effect immediately.
Is posting business ideas free?
Yes! Posting business ideas is completely free on all plans. You only need reveals to view contact information of other posters.
