Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 13, 2026

At BizzStop ("we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website www.bizzstop.com ("Platform").

By using BizzStop, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile details when you register
  • Business Information: Business ideas, partnership preferences, and any content you post on the Platform
  • Communication Data: Messages exchanged with other users and customer support inquiries

Payment Information

We use Paddle as our Merchant of Record for payment processing. Paddle collects and processes payment information (credit card details, billing address) on our behalf. We do not store full payment card information on our servers.

Usage Data

  • IP address, browser type, operating system

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage patterns, and remember your preferences.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Platform

  • Send important updates, notifications, and promotional materials (you can opt out)

  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Analyze usage trends and optimize platform performance

3. AI-Powered Features

BizzStop uses artificial intelligence (AI) tools to provide features such as:

  • Business idea generators
  • Pitch generators
  • Financial projection templates
  • Smart matchmaking algorithms

When you use these features, your inputs may be processed by third-party AI providers (e.g., OpenAI). These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not share personally identifiable information with AI providers unless necessary for service delivery.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

With Other Users

Your profile information, business ideas, and partnership preferences are visible to other registered users to facilitate connections.

With Service Providers

  • Paddle: For payment processing and subscription management

  • Hosting Providers: For platform infrastructure and data storage

For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, or to protect our rights and safety.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@bizzstop.com.

However, no online platform is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

BizzStop is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will delete it immediately.
